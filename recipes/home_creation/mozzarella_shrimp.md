# Mozzarella Shrimp

#Protein

## Cooking Time

- Active Cooking Time: 5 minutes
- Total Cooking Time: 20 minutes
- Preparation Time: 5 minutes

## Ingredients

- Shrimp [250 grams]
- Garlic [3 units]
- Olive oil
- Mozzarella cheese
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Rinse the shrimp and toss with a little salt, black pepper, and lemon juice. Marinate for 5 minutes.
2. Finely mince the garlic. Heat the olive oil, add the garlic, and stir to make garlic-infused oil.
3. Arrange the shrimp in a single layer in a baking dish. Toss with the garlic, then cover generously with shredded mozzarella cheese.
4. Preheat the oven to 180°C. Bake until the cheese is melted and golden and the shrimp are cooked through.
