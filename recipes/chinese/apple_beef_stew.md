
# Apple Beef Stew

#Vegetables_Protein

## Cooking Time

- Active Cooking Time: 20 minutes
- Total Cooking Time: 70 minutes

## Ingredients

- Beef [1000 grams]
- Onion [1 unit]
- Carrot [3 units]
- Apple [1 unit]
- Soy sauce
- Black pepper
- Red wine
- Cinnamon
- Bay leaves
- Oyster sauce
- Saltß
- Rosemary

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a pot and sauté a generous amount of onion until fragrant.
2. Add the beef and carrot and stir-fry until the beef is partially cooked.
3. Add soy sauce and a generous amount of black pepper. Stir to combine.
4. Add the apple and stir-fry until evenly mixed.
5. Add the red wine, cinnamon, bay leaves, and oyster sauce.
6. Cover and simmer for at least 45 minutes, adjusting the cooking time according to the size and tenderness of the beef pieces.
7. Uncover and cook over high heat to reduce the sauce. Season with salt to taste, then sprinkle with rosemary before serving.

## Extension

- If red wine is unavailable, regular Chinese cooking wine can be used instead.
