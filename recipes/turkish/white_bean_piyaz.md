# White Bean Piyaz

#Vegetables #Protein

## Cooking Time

- Active Cooking Time: 20 minutes
- Total Cooking Time: 90 minutes
- Preparation Time: 8 hours (overnight soak)

## Ingredients

- Dried beans [1 to 1 1/2 cups]
- Tomatoes [3-4]
- Green peppers [10-15]
- Fresh green onions [1 bunch] or dry onion [1]
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Vinegar
- Hot red pepper (optional)

## Instructions

1. The night before, soak the dried beans in plenty of water and let them sit overnight.
2. The next day, boil the beans until they are soft enough to eat.
3. Drain the beans and place them in a deep bowl.
4. Finely chop the fresh green onion, or dry onion (rubbed with salt and cut into small piyaz-style pieces), along with the tomatoes and green peppers, and add them to the beans.
5. Add olive oil, salt, and vinegar, mix everything together, and serve.

## Extension

- If you use dry onion rubbed with salt, there is no need to add extra salt.
- Add finely chopped hot red pepper for a spicier version.
